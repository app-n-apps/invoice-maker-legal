Support

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Developer: app-n-apps

Need help with Invoice Maker? Contact app-n-apps at playnowthisgame@gmail.com for questions about invoices, estimates, PDFs, or your subscription.

Contact support

Describe what happened, what you expected, and the steps that led to the issue. If relevant, include your app version and iOS version. Do not send passwords, payment card details, or Apple ID credentials.

Your documents

Documents are stored on your device only, so we cannot recover them if the app is deleted. Share or save PDF copies of anything you need to keep. Creating a PDF may need an internet connection to download fonts.

Subscription and billing

To recover access to an existing subscription, use Restore Purchases in the app. To manage renewal, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Invoice Maker. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Apple handles billing and refund requests.
