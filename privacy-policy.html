Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Developer: app-n-apps

Invoice Maker is an invoice and estimate app developed by app-n-apps. This policy explains what data stays on your device and what is received by the services the app uses.

Your documents and business details

The business details, clients, invoices, estimates, and settings you enter are stored locally on your device. Invoice Maker has no user accounts and we do not receive this information. A PDF leaves your device only when you choose to share, send, or print it, through the iOS share sheet or print dialog and the app or service you select.

Subscriptions

Apple processes all payments; we do not receive your payment card details. Invoice Maker uses Apphud to manage subscriptions. Apphud receives and retains purchase and subscription information, an app user ID generated for your install, device identifiers such as IDFV, and technical information such as device model, iOS version, and app version. Apphud processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Fonts for PDF generation

When the app creates a PDF, it may download font files from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com). As with any network request, Google receives your device's IP address. No document content is sent with this request.

Data we do not collect

Invoice Maker does not access your location, contacts, camera, or photo library. It does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, does not record your screen, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Deleting Invoice Maker from your device removes all documents and details stored by the app. To request deletion of subscription data held by Apphud, email playnowthisgame@gmail.com. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; manage cancellation in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

Invoice Maker is rated 4+ and is a business tool. It is not intended to collect data from children.

Your rights and questions

For questions about your privacy rights or help with a data request, contact app-n-apps at playnowthisgame@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

Changes to this policy are published on this page. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when this policy was last revised.
